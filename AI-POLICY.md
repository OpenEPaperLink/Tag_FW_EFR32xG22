# Use of AI tools when interacting with this project and its maintainers / community

The emergence of a rapidly-evolving ecosystem of AI-driven and AI-assisted tools has undeniably changed the open source landscape. While its merits on lowering the barrier of entry are undeniable, there are still human factors involved in the creation and maintenance of an open source project such as this one. Therefore, the following rules apply here:

1. **AI usage needs to be disclosed**

If AI was meaningfully involved in the creation of your contribution, it needs to be disclosed. This can be a note, a co-author tag, or a mention in the PR.

2. **Human interaction stays human**

Humans are maintaining this project. If you are interacting with a human, you are required to be a human too. Nothing wears down a maintainer faster than having to wade through endless lines of AI-generated prose, which in the end does not convey more relevant information than the original prompt contained.

This also covers the use of AI to 'clean up' or 'flesh out' posts. It's easier to discern your human reasoning from your original thoughts versus a wall of text that was auto-generated from a oneliner you put in. This is true even for AI-assisted translations - if you do not have basic understanding of the language you are translating to, how can you know the translation covers your original intent?

3. **A human is responsible for any contribution made**

No fully automatic AI submissions, period. If you have a question, ask in your own words - you are expected to have put in reasonable effort to understand what you're asking and why. If you have a contribution, you are expected to be able to explain what it is for, what it does, how it fits into the existing project, and why you think it is ready for acceptance.
